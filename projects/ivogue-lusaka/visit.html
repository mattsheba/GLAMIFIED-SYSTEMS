Before you come

What to bring

Buying

Your NRC or passport if you are paying by card, and the phone that holds your mobile money wallet. We'll set the phone up, move your data across and fit the screen protector before you leave.

Trading in

The old phone, its Apple ID password, and the charger and box if you still have them. Sign out of iCloud before you come if you can — it saves about ten minutes.

Collecting an order

Your order reference and the NRC of the person named on the order. Someone else collecting? Send us their name on WhatsApp first.

Repairs and warranty

The receipt we gave you — it carries the IMEI and the warranty terms. No receipt? We can look the IMEI up in our records.

Can't make it into town?

Order online before 15:00 and a rider brings it anywhere in Lusaka the same day, insured. Rest of Zambia goes by courier in 1–3 working days.
