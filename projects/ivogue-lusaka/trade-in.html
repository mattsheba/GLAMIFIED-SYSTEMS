How the trade-in works

Do I have to buy something to trade in?
No. You can sell the phone outright and we pay you by Airtel, MTN or Zamtel the same day. You get a better figure when the value goes against a phone in the shop, because we skip the cash-out cost.
What makes the value drop?
Cracked glass, a battery under 80%, a failed Face ID, a replaced non-genuine screen, water damage, or an iCloud lock. We show you each deduction on screen before you agree to anything.
What do you do with my data?
The phone is signed out of iCloud and erased in front of you before it leaves the counter. Nothing is kept, and nothing is resold before it has been wiped and re-tested.
My screen is cracked — is it worth bringing?
Yes. A cracked iPhone still has value; the Damaged grade on this calculator is the figure we work from. Bring it in rather than guessing.
