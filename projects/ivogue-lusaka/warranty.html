The fine print, in plain words

Warranty, returns & delivery

Last updated 31 October 2026. This is the policy we actually apply at the counter — nothing hidden in a longer document somewhere else.

Warranty

Sealed iPhone, Apple Watch and AirPods — 12 months
Covers manufacturing faults: the device not powering on, a screen that fails on its own, a camera or speaker that stops working, a charging port that dies, or a battery that drops below 80% health within the year. Bring the device and the receipt. We test it, and repair or replace it. Where the fault is covered by Apple's own warranty we handle that claim for you.
Certified pre-owned handsets — 90 days
Parts and labour on anything that was working when you bought it and stops working on its own within 90 days. The battery health we printed on your receipt is what we stand behind; if it falls more than 5 percentage points below that figure inside 90 days we replace the battery free.
Accessories
Cases and cables: 3–6 months against splitting, fraying or a failed connector. Chargers and power banks: 12 months. Fitted screen protectors: we re-fit free if dust or a bubble gets under it, any time.
What the warranty does not cover
Cracked or chipped glass, bent frames, liquid damage, damage from a non-genuine charger, software problems caused by jailbreaking, and any device opened by somebody else. Water resistance is a factory rating, not a promise — it is not covered once the phone has been dropped or repaired.

Returns and swaps

  • 7 days to return an unopened, unregistered item with the receipt for a full refund by the method you paid with.
  • Opened sealed items are exchange-or-repair, not refund. Once an iPhone is activated it is no longer sealed stock and cannot be resold as new.
  • 48-hour swap on certified pre-owned handsets: if it misbehaves in the first two days, bring it back and swap it for another unit of equal value.
  • Mobile money refunds go back to the wallet that paid, usually within 24 hours. Card refunds take 3–10 working days depending on your bank.
  • Nothing here removes your rights under the Competition and Consumer Protection Act No. 24 of 2010.

Delivery & collection

  • Order before 15:00 on a trading day for same-day delivery inside Lusaka.
  • Every delivery is insured until it is in your hands. The rider calls before arriving.
  • We check the NRC or order reference of whoever receives the parcel on high-value items.
  • Collection at East Park Mall is free and usually ready within the hour.
  • Nothing ships before payment clears, except reserved collections, which we hold for 24 hours.

Stolen or blocked handsets

Every handset we buy or sell has its IMEI recorded and checked. We do not buy a phone without seeing the seller's NRC, and we will not take a phone that is iCloud-locked or reported stolen. If you believe a phone you bought here has a history problem, bring it in with the receipt and we will resolve it the same day.

Message us with your order reference and we will sort it out.
